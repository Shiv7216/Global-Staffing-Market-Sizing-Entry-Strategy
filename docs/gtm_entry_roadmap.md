# Phased Go-To-Market (GTM) Expansion Roadmap

## Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Tier-1 Corridor Entry (US & UK)
- **Target Verticals:** Enterprise IT/Cloud Engineering & Allied Healthcare Staffing.
- **Channel Strategy:** Partner with mid-market MSP/VMS vendors and regional hospital networks facing acute clinician and software talent shortages.
- **Unit Economics Target:** 22.8%-24.5% gross bill-to-pay margin spread with <45 day credentialing turnaround.

## Phase 2 (Months 7-12): European & APAC Scale-Up (Germany, Australia, Ireland)
- **Target Verticals:** Industrial Automation, Data Engineering, and Geriatric/Allied Care.
- **Regulatory Execution:** Leverage bilateral visa frameworks and EOR (Employer of Record) compliance partners.
